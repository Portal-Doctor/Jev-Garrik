## 0. Common rules
- **What a pause means:** the class drops to minimum size ($20 risk) and keeps trading in paper. It does not stop, so forward evidence keeps accumulating.
  - Pauses never close open positions, except the Jev exit guard in shadow, which only logs its exits.
  - Every pause and resume is logged with its timestamp, trigger and values.
- **Hard limits:** registered halts (§6) and the 20R class kill are unchanged. They take precedence over everything here.
- **Evidence:** a class's R count includes minimum-size trades, measured in R, so the evidence stays continuous.

## 1. Quant's statistical guards
**1a. SPRT (H0 +0.25R vs H1 0, alpha = beta = 0.2): report only, never acts.**
- At a per-trade SD of about 1.5R, its expected sample under H1 is about 100 trades.
- A normal likelihood is also wrong for positive-skew trend R, and alpha = 0.2 would pause a true +0.25R class 1 time in 5.
- Reported at every review with its expected-n note.

**1b. Fast bleed pause (acts):** pause when the rolling 20-trade net R sum is <= -5R (a mean of -0.25R).
- Under H0 (+0.25R, SD 1.5R): about a 7% false-alarm rate per independent window.
- At a true E of -0.25R: it fires about 50% of the time.
- **Resume:** back to full size when, at minimum size, the next 20 trades have a net R sum >= 0, OR Brian approves after review.

**1c. Class drawdown pause (acts):** pause when class net drawdown from peak is >= 10R. It sits under the 20R kill.
- **Resume:** back to full size once the class recovers 5R from its trough (half the trigger), OR Brian approves.
- The 20R kill is measured from the same peak, so a slow grind continues to the kill.

**1d. Ex-top-3 (Architect item f): FLAG and report only for A and B, never a kill input.**
- A positive-skew trend or momentum class is expected to be negative without its top 3 trades at n = 30, so a kill on that basis would kill A by design.
- The kill for A and B stays: the registered kill screen, plus at n >= 30 forward, mean net R < 0 AND the net $/month L80 < 0.
- **FLAGs:**
  - more than 80% of gross from 2 or fewer pairs;
  - the largest single trade is more than 60% of total net;
  - ex-top-3 net < 0 together with median net R < -0.5R.

**1e. Always report:** median net R, hit rate vs the payoff-implied break-even, ex-top-3 net, the largest single trade's share of net, and the top-3 capture.

## 2. Trader's market-level triggers (non-Jev, acting rules)
**2a. Breadth pause (A and B new entries):** no new A/B entries when fewer than 25% of eligible universe names close above their daily EMA 50 (UTC daily close).
- Resume above 35% (hysteresis). Open positions keep their class exits.
- Pre-hash geometry reports the fire rate over the 1-year snapshot. If it is above 30% of days, flag it to Brian. Never retune it.

**2b. Momentum-crash guard (B only):** BTC-USD 60-day return <= -25% AND 10-day return >= +15%.
- B goes to minimum size and adds no new names for 14 days. Holdings keep their normal exits.

**2c. No separate BTC 7-day drawdown trigger.** The registered BTC veto plus breadth already cover falling markets.

**D coverage (Architect question):** the BTC veto already covers D. 2a and 2b do not apply to D (it's forward-only at minimum size anyway). The Jev guards do not cover D either.

## 3. Jev guards (SHADOW ONLY in v3.1, logged, no authority)
**Base-rate problem (Architect item a):**
- toxic_flow = high on about 89% of repo decisions, and stressed on about 68%. Neither is significant.
- Absolute thresholds would fire constantly, so v3.1 uses each pair's own trailing distribution.
- Packet as frozen in gap 18 (Fallback B, position = 0). All 4 questions in one call. Reads every completed 15m bar.

**3a. Jev exit guard (open A/B positions), shadow:**
- Fires when the pair's P(toxic_flow = high) AND P(liquidity_stress = stressed) are both >= that pair's trailing 30-day 95th percentile of Jev reads, on 3 consecutive completed 15m reads.
  - Because the reads overlap (4h horizon), 3 reads means about 45 minutes of persistence, not 3 independent signals.
- **Only while the open trade is at or below +1R unrealized.** Past +1R, the registered trail governs, which protects the tail winners.
- Shadow exit at taker (90 + s_bt) at the next 15m open.
- **Warm-up:** the guard is undefined until a pair has 30 days of forward Jev reads. No fires before that; it's logged as "warming".

**3b. Jev market pause (BTC-USD book), shadow (Architect item b):**
- The regime question has no risk-off option. `cb/model.ts` lists expansion ("directional, volatile tape"), balance, and contraction ("volatility compressing or the tape is quiet"). Contraction is close to the opposite of risk-off, and mapping any of them onto risk-off is the label inversion §9 bans. **The regime label is not used as a trigger.** It is logged only.
- The trigger uses the literal stress labels instead. It fires when BTC-USD's P(toxic high) AND P(stressed) are both >= their trailing 30-day 99th percentile on 2 consecutive reads.
- Shadow effect: it blocks new A/B entries for 4h after the last firing read.

**3c. Re-entry (Architect item c):** a guard exit counts as a stop for the 24h no-re-entry rule, in both the shadow and the guarded twin.

**3d. Degenerate-guard fail (pre-set):**
- If 3a fires on more than 10% of position-days, or 3b blocks more than 15% of hours, over the first 60 days after warm-up, that guard is "not testable / uninformative".
- It is never retuned within v3.1.

**3e. Validation (for any future authority, which needs a new registration plus Brian):**
- At least 30 triggers per guard.
- The guarded twin beats the unguarded twin by more than the taker cost.
- It also beats a random-exit placebo at the same trigger rate. Placebo draws come only from bars where the trade is at or below +1R, the same constraint the guard faces.
- **Top-3 capture:** if the guard gives up more than 25% of the unguarded twin's top-3 gross, it fails regardless of the loss saved.

**3f. Jev Holm family (Architect item g):** a separate family of 2 (3a and 3b). The registered forward Jev family of 4 stays as frozen and isn't diluted. Quant to confirm.

**3g. Cost:** at worst 8 positions plus BTC x 96 reads a day is about $0.40 a month. Not binding.

## 4. Cluster cap (joint position to confirm)
- **Problem:** single linkage at rho >= 0.8 on 60-day daily returns chains most crypto alts into one cluster. "At most 2 per cluster" then caps the whole book at about 2-3 positions (Architect's "realistic 2-3"), so the 8-slot plan never binds. [Quant edit: not borne out by P5. The capped P5 book held a mean of 3.9 open positions (max 7, ≥ 3 open in 84% of hours). The cluster gate blocked 30 of 238 attempts (29 B), against 94 for slots and 125 for one-per-pair. The cap binds mainly on B in Jan–Apr. See Quant review Q4.]
- **Proposal:** **average linkage** at rho >= 0.80, recomputed monthly, with **at most 3 open positions per cluster**. The beta-weighted notional cap ($6K, 60-day beta) stays as the portfolio backstop.
- Pre-hash geometry, as a report with no tuning:
  - the number of clusters and the largest cluster's share of the universe per month;
  - the realized max concurrent positions under the cap.
  - If the largest cluster holds more than 60% of eligible names on more than half the months, flag it to Brian.

### Q1. Statistical guards (§1)
**1a. SPRT report-only: ACCEPT.**
- Expected n to reach H1 (Wald, ignoring overshoot) = ln((1−β)/α) / (Δ²/2σ²) = ln 4 / (0.0625 / 2σ²).
  - σ = 1.5R → **100 trades**; σ = 1.0R → **44 trades**.
- It is a slow backstop, and it never acts.

**1b. Rolling-20 pause at ≤ −5R: ACCEPT. Arithmetic verified, with one caveat for Brian.**
- **Per window** (normal, σ = 1.5R; the 20-trade sum has mean 20E and SD 1.5 × √20 = 6.71R):

  | True E | Chance one window fires | Note |
  |---|---|---|
  | +0.25R | **6.8%** | z = −1.49 |
  | 0 | 22.8% | |
  | −0.25R | **50.0%** | |

- **Caveat:** windows roll trade by trade and overlap, so the chance of *at least one* pause grows with n (simulated, 20,000 runs):

  | True E | Model | by 20 trades | by 40 | by 60 | by 100 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | +0.25R | normal, σ 1.5R | 7% | 26% | 40% | **62%** |
  | +0.25R | skewed, σ ≈ 2.3R (35% winners) | 16% | 48% | 69% | **88%** |
  | −0.25R | normal | 50% | **87%** | 97% | – |

- A healthy +0.25R class will **probably be paused at least once in its first 100 trades**. That is acceptable only because a pause means minimum size, never stopping, and it resumes after 20 trades with a net sum ≥ 0.
  - Realized σ is lower for B (P5 B: σ = 1.09R, skew 2.2, n = 51), which cuts the single-window false alarm to about 2%. A's σ is higher (1.70R, n = 10).
- **Resume (20 trades at minimum size with a net sum ≥ 0, or Brian's OK): ACCEPT.**

**1c. 10R pause, 5R-recovery resume, 20R kill from the same peak: ACCEPT.**

**1d. Ex-top-3 as a FLAG only for A/B; kill = mean net R < 0 AND net $/month L80 < 0 at forward n ≥ 30: ACCEPT.** This supersedes Quant's earlier "ex-top-3 net < 0 AND L80 < 0" kill. It's cleaner, and it doesn't kill a genuine positive-skew class by design. The concentration flags (> 80% of gross from ≤ 2 pairs; one trade > 60% of net) are kept.

**1e. Reporting: ACCEPT.**

### Q4. Cluster cap (§4): ACCEPT Trader's rule, with two specifications and one fact fix
**Fact fix ([Quant edit] in §4):** the registered rule didn't cap the P5 book at 2–3 positions.
- The P5 capped book held a **mean of 3.9 open positions** (Jan–Apr 3.2, May–Oct 4.6), a **max of 7**, and **≥ 3 open in 84% of hours**.
- The cluster gate blocked **30 of 238 entry attempts** (29 of them B). Slots blocked 94 (A 32, B 62), and one-per-pair blocked 125.
- So the cap binds in the Jan–Apr high-correlation regime, mainly on B. It isn't what holds the book down.

**Replication of the clusters** (60-day daily-return ρ ≥ 0.8, monthly, 2026-01-01 → 2026-10-01, 10 months):
- Clustering over **all 50 ever-members** reproduces P5's largest clusters almost exactly (34/33/37/40/27… vs P5's 33/32/39/40/26…).
- So **P5 clustered over names outside the current universe**, and non-members can chain members together.

| Month | Members | Avg ρ | Single: clusters / largest share | Average: clusters / largest share | Eigen effective bets (whole universe) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jan | 40 | 0.62 | 11 / 75% | 20 / 45% | 2.2 |
| Feb | 40 | 0.67 | 13 / 70% | 18 / 50% | 2.1 |
| Mar | 34 | 0.74 | 7 / 79% | 10 / **68%** | 1.7 |
| Apr | 30 | 0.74 | 6 / 83% | 12 / 60% | 1.7 |
| May | 28 | 0.64 | 11 / 64% | 16 / 46% | 2.1 |
| Jun | 26 | 0.54 | 16 / 42% | 18 / 31% | 2.8 |
| Jul | 26 | 0.53 | 15 / 46% | 19 / 31% | 2.9 |

| Sep | 24 | 0.59 | 15 / 42% | 18 / 17% | 2.5 |
| Oct | 27 | 0.48 | 20 / 30% | 23 / 15% | 3.4 |

The table is the members-only basis. Eigen effective bets = (Σλ)²/Σλ² of the member correlation matrix.

**Largest cluster above 60% of names:**
- Single linkage: 5 of 10 months (all-50 basis: 4 of 10).
- Average linkage: **1 of 10** (March; all-50 basis: 0 of 10).
- So **Trader's > 60%-on-more-than-half-the-months flag would not trip** on this year.

**Capacity:**
- The sum of min(cap, cluster size) is ≥ 7 every month under both rules, so the 8 slots are never limited by cluster *count*.
- What binds is that the candidates concentrate in the big cluster:
  - Registered single linkage with 2 per cluster allows **2 open names in the big cluster**, which held 64–83% of names Jan–May.
  - Average linkage with 3 per cluster allows **3 in a cluster** that holds ≤ 50% of names in 8 of 10 months.
- In a weekly class-B replay (top 20% by score, k = 4), the registered rule cut the 4-name book in 4 of 37 weeks (Jan 12, Mar 9–30). Average linkage with 3 per cluster cut it in 0 weeks.

**Effective independent bets for n equal-risk positions,** n_eff = n / (1 + (n−1)ρ̄):

| ρ̄ | n = 2 | n = 3 | n = 4 | n = 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.75 (Mar–Apr) | 1.14 | 1.20 | 1.23 | 1.28 |
| 0.60 (year average) | 1.25 | 1.36 | 1.43 | 1.54 |
| 0.48 (Oct) | 1.35 | 1.53 | 1.64 | 1.83 |

- The whole universe only carries about **1.7–3.4 independent bets** (eigenvalue count), even with unlimited slots.
- **Trade-off:** extra slots in a high-correlation cluster speed up the *trade count* but add almost no *independent evidence*. At ρ 0.75, going from 2 to 3 positions adds 0.06 effective bets.
  - They also add crash exposure: in a correlated gap, every position hits its stop together.
  - The weekly-block bootstrap and the forward review should therefore count evidence in weeks, not just trades. Concurrent trades in one cluster are not independent.

**Rule for v3.1:**
- **ACCEPT** average linkage at ρ ≥ 0.80, ≤ 3 open per cluster, recomputed monthly, with Trader's > 60% flag.
- **Specifications [pending Trader]:**
  - (i) **Cluster only over the current universe members** at that rebalance. Non-members never link names.
  - (ii) A name with fewer than 60 in-window days is its own cluster (as registered).
- **Binding control stays the $480 portfolio open-risk cap** (8 × $60), plus the $6K beta-weighted notional cap. The cluster rule only limits pile-ups. The open-risk cap is what bounds a correlated crash: at worst a gap through all stops costs the open risk plus gap slippage, which the $900/$8K backstops cover.
- **Not adopted:** the "Σ risk × √ρ̄ ≤ $480" form. At $60 a position it allows n ≤ 8/√ρ̄, which is ≥ 9 at any ρ̄ ≤ 0.8, so it never binds.
