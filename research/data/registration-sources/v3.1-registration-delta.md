## 1. Feasibility verdict: FEASIBLE, with 6 specifications Senior must build to
S1. **Jev reads on every universe member, not just open positions.** The per-pair trailing 30-day percentiles (3a) and pooled percentiles (warm-up) need a read history on every pair. Poll Fallback B (frozen gap-18 packet, position = 0, 4 questions in one call) for all current members plus BTC-USD at each completed 15m bar. That's about 40 x 96 x 30 = 115k calls/month, roughly $1.80/month at $0.0000155 per call, still trivial against $100. It also needs a continuous public L2 and trades subscription for every member so the MarketState fields (spread EMA, imbalance, RSI, returns path) stay warm. That runs fine on the home PC. Rebuild state after a restart, and log reads during rebuild as "warming". The stale-feed kill stays scoped to held pairs, as registered.
S2. **Guard twins are per-position counterfactuals.** For 3a, the guarded twin and the placebo exit the same position at the shadow exit bar. They don't re-simulate the portfolio, so freed slots and cap headroom are not reused. The 3c re-entry block applies to that pair inside the twin only. This keeps the comparison paired, per TDF §9's ablation design.
S3. **Units for R-based gates.** 1b, 1c and the 20R class kill run on the class's cumulative net R curve, with minimum-size trades counted in R (package §0). The registered 20R kill is restated in R units; the "$1,200" in TDF §6 was 20R at $60 and stays as a dollar backstop alongside it. Under minimum-size trading this is conservative (it kills sooner). The A/B kill's "$/month L80" stays in dollars as written.
S4. **Breadth (2a) denominator:** current members whose daily EMA50 is defined. Boundary-saved names use D1 closes for EMA50, per the D1 ruling. Names with an undefined EMA50 are left out of both numerator and denominator. Measured at the 00:00 UTC daily close and applied to the next UTC day's entries. Point-in-time membership.
S5. **Cluster input:** clustering runs over current members only (Q4 spec i). 60-day daily returns from 15m-derived closes in the window, D1 closes before it per the D1 ruling. A name with under 60 days is its own cluster. Average linkage (UPGMA) at rho >= 0.80. Recompute on the first UTC day of the month and at fold starts. An existing position keeps its cluster tag until it exits.
S6. **Precedence:** registered halts, backstops, the 20R kill and the kill switches come first, then the pauses (1b, 1c, 2a, 2b), then the class rules. When several pauses overlap the effect doesn't compound: the class sits at $20 minimum, with no new entries while 2a, or 2b for B, is on.

### 5b. p-values on NET, not gross
- TDF §8 gate 2 is defined on mean **net R** at Coinbase 50/90. Gate 3 (Holm) and gate 4 (max-of-N) test the same quantity within the family. TDF §1: "Results are realized net R and net bps only." TDF §7: the metric is net $ after fees. Gate 5 (deflated Sharpe) is the Sharpe of the same net series.
- Ruling: Holm, BH, max-of-N, DSR and the bootstrap CIs are all computed on **realized net R per trade** at Coinbase Intro 50/90 with base s_bt. The same stats are reported again at the 2x s_bt stress row. Gross is used **only** in the ADD §5 kill screen (mean gross and U80 gross vs the hurdle) and in the gross columns. Computing p on gross is a code fix, not an amendment.

### 5c. Registered-rule code fixes (not amendments; registration classes.A = "Design section 3")
- F1 A chase skip (TDF §3 A): skip if the X1 entry price, meaning the next 1h open after the 00:00 UTC signal, is above signal close + 0.5 x daily ATR14. Not "signal close vs prior high".
- F2 A 10-day exit (TDF §3 A): a daily close below the lowest daily **close** of the prior 10 days. Exit taker at the **next open** (the first 15m open after that 00:00 UTC close) at open - s_bt. Not lowest low, and not the last 15m close.
- F3 Stop trigger (TDF §1, gap 17, ADD §4): the trigger is the **stop price**. A bar whose low is <= the stop is stopped. The fill is stop - max(s_bt, 0.1 x 1h ATR14), one deduction. If the bar **opens** at or below the stop, the fill is the open (gap rule). Stop is checked first when stop and target share a bar.
- F4 Kill hurdle (ADD §5 kill screen, TDF §1): hurdle per class = mean over its trades of C_i + max(25, 0.15 x S_i) bps, where C_i is the trade's realized cost (190 base for X1 taker/taker). Remove the hard-coded 150.
- F5 20R class kill (TDF §6, S3 above): implemented in the book, measured on the class's cumulative net R curve from peak, with $1,200 as a dollar backstop.
- F6 Book halt (TDF §6 "-$1,600 (20%) research drawdown"): drawdown from peak equity (R3: realized plus open MTM at each 15m close), not cumulative P&L. The same basis applies to the $8K backstop.
- F7 B rebalance timing (TDF §3 B plus X1 §4): the signal is at the Monday 00:00 UTC close and the X1 entry is the next 1h open (Monday 01:00 UTC), not Tuesday.
- F8 F5 flags: P5 reads the registered priceGroupFailNames / volume flags from the registration. It doesn't recompute them at runtime (QA probe).
- F9 Write guard: `cli p5` calls requireFrozenWrites() / assertFrozenRoots / assertCleanWorkTree like every other writer. On a fresh clone with no data it must refuse and write nothing.
- F10 Unit tests with synthetic fixtures for: open-risk cap, gross cap, beta cap, cluster cap (average linkage, 3 per cluster, members only, <60-day singleton), daily/weekly/book halts on equity, backstops, 20R kill, 24h re-entry, F1-F3 edge cases (QA's probes: low between the worsened price and the stop; a bar opening below the stop), and A's per-fold selection with the purge.
